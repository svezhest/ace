## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00002] helpful=1 harmful=0 :: Do NOT default to 'PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross' for every acquisition-related dollar amount. This tag represents the TOTAL GROSS PAYMENTS (including cash, debt, and equity) to acquire a business, and is broader. For a specific business combination, the more precise tag 'BusinessCombinationConsiderationTransferred1' captures the specific consideration transferred. Avoid relying on surface-level keywords like 'purchase price', 'acquisition', or 'consideration' alone; instead, understand the precise accounting context and the specific role of each tag, and establish a consistent decision framework rather than applying one tag to all similar cases.

## CONTEXT CLUES & INDICATORS

[ctx-00001] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When an entity issues shares, first determine its ROLE in the transaction before matching a tag. If the entity is the ACQUIRER issuing shares as CONSIDERATION to acquire a business, use 'BusinessAcquisitionEquityInterestsIssuedOrIssuableNumberOfSharesIssued'. If the entity is issuing its OWN shares in its own capacity (to raise capital, for employee compensation, or in a secondary offering), use 'SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction'. Context clues that signal acquisition consideration: phrases like 'in exchange for', 'tendered units', 'exchange offer', 'common units exchanged for common stock', and the explicit mention of a target business being acquired. The critical distinction is that the acquirer counts shares ISSUED as consideration, not shares SOLD by itself.
[ctx-00004] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a company describes selling newly issued shares to investors in a PRIMARY OFFERING (with underwriters, overallotments, or investment banks), these ARE new shares issued by the company, not sales of existing shares. The correct tag is 'StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues', which is the standard XBRL tag for shares issued during a period. Do NOT default to 'SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction' just because the language uses 'sold' - in a primary offering context, 'sold' means the company issued new shares to investors. The key distinction: 'SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction' is for secondary offerings where existing shareholders sell their shares, while 'StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues' is for primary offerings where the company issues new shares. Rely on the standard taxonomy structure and the actual transaction type rather than over-interpreting the language and creating distinctions that don't align with the taxonomy. When in doubt, check whether the company is issuing NEW shares (primary) or selling EXISTING shares (secondary) to determine the correct tag.
## OTHERS
[misc-00003] helpful=0 harmful=1 :: Decision framework for acquisition-related entities: STEP 1 - Determine the entity's ROLE: is it issuing equity AS consideration to acquire a business (→ BusinessAcquisitionEquityInterestsIssuedOrIssuableNumberOfSharesIssued), or selling its own stock (→ SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction)? STEP 2 - For dollar amounts, decide between 'PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross' (total gross payments including cash, debt, equity) and 'BusinessCombinationConsiderationTransferred1' (specific consideration transferred). STEP 3 - Apply the specificity principle: when a specific business-combination consideration tag exists and the amount represents the consideration transferred for that business combination, prefer the specific tag over the broader payments tag. Do not let a bias toward one tag override the accounting context.
