## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00002] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do NOT default to 'PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross' for every acquisition-related dollar amount. This tag represents the TOTAL GROSS PAYMENTS (including cash, debt, and equity) to acquire a business, and is broader. For a specific business combination, the more precise tag 'BusinessCombinationConsiderationTransferred1' captures the specific consideration transferred. Avoid relying on surface-level keywords like 'purchase price', 'acquisition', or 'consideration' alone; instead, understand the precise accounting context and the specific role of each tag, and establish a consistent decision framework rather than applying one tag to all similar cases.

## CONTEXT CLUES & INDICATORS

[ctx-00001] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When an entity issues shares, first determine its ROLE in the transaction before matching a tag. If the entity is the ACQUIRER issuing shares as CONSIDERATION to acquire a business, use 'BusinessAcquisitionEquityInterestsIssuedOrIssuableNumberOfSharesIssued'. If the entity is issuing its OWN shares in its own capacity (to raise capital, for employee compensation, or in a secondary offering), use 'SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction'. Context clues that signal acquisition consideration: phrases like 'in exchange for', 'tendered units', 'exchange offer', 'common units exchanged for common stock', and the explicit mention of a target business being acquired. The critical distinction is that the acquirer counts shares ISSUED as consideration, not shares SOLD by itself.
## OTHERS
[misc-00003] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Decision framework for acquisition-related entities: STEP 1 - Determine the entity's ROLE: is it issuing equity AS consideration to acquire a business (→ BusinessAcquisitionEquityInterestsIssuedOrIssuableNumberOfSharesIssued), or selling its own stock (→ SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction)? STEP 2 - For dollar amounts, decide between 'PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross' (total gross payments including cash, debt, equity) and 'BusinessCombinationConsiderationTransferred1' (specific consideration transferred). STEP 3 - Apply the specificity principle: when a specific business-combination consideration tag exists and the amount represents the consideration transferred for that business combination, prefer the specific tag over the broader payments tag. Do not let a bias toward one tag override the accounting context.
